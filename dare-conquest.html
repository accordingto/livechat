🏰 Dare Conquest

🎚️ Question difficulty
Each player takes turns rolling the dice and moving their token around the board. Land on a plain tile → do the dare shown on screen. Pull it off and the land is yours — even if a rival already owns it, so the board keeps changing hands even after every tile is claimed. A few tiles are special and can't be claimed: START and LUCKY both swap all your territory with another player's, and BONUS and GIFT both take 2 territories off whoever is leading.
