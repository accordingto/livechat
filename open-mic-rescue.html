Give singing a little excuse.

One player takes a short social challenge. Pass for +2, then sing for +1 or simply skip. Miss the challenge? A song can rescue +1.

No voice judging. No mandatory performances. Just a shared team score.

How to play

The Spotlight player tries the challenge while everyone chats, asks questions, and reacts. The host chooses Success or Failed.

Success adds +2 to your team. Singing a short section adds +1 after either result. Skipping is always okay.

Browse and preview songs, pick one you know, then start the 35-second visual timer. At zero, finish the phrase naturally. The host moves to the next player.

Sing through your usual call or in the room. YouTube playback stays on each device; share computer audio if you want the group to hear the music.
