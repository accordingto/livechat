2–9 players · Your usual voice call

How to play

The Spotlight player tries the challenge while everyone chats, asks questions, and reacts. The host chooses Success or Failed.

Success adds +2 to your team. Complete a short original creation or story for +1 after either result. Skipping is always okay.

Choose a text prompt or song name, then use the 35-second timer to share a memory or create an original tune or rhythm. Finish naturally; the host moves to the next player.

Speak or create through your usual call or in the room. This game does not play videos or display lyrics. YouTube links open separately when you choose them.
