THE GAME

這是什麼遊戲？What's the game?

一個人拿到一張祕密單字卡，要用英文讓大家猜出這個字——但卡片上列出的禁字一個都不能說！ One player gets a secret word card and describes it in English so others can guess — but every forbidden word on the card is off-limits!

不用開鏡頭、不用下載 App，每個人用自己的手機打開專屬連結就能玩。英文不用很好，簡單的字就夠了，越卡越好笑 😂 No camera, no app download — just open your own private link on your phone. Simple English is all you need, and the struggle is half the fun.

「它是一種水果……外面硬硬的……頭上戴著一頂皇冠——啊！不能說皇冠！」 "It's a fruit… hard on the outside… it wears a crown— oops, can't say crown!"

ROLES

每一輪三種角色Three roles every round

描述者Clue giver

看著祕密單字，用英文描述它，避開所有禁字。Sees the secret word and describes it in English — avoiding every forbidden word.

裁判Referee

也看得到禁字。描述者一說出禁字，馬上按 🚨 Taboo! 抓包！Also sees the forbidden words. The moment the clue giver says one, hit 🚨 Taboo!

猜題者Guessers

看不到題目，只靠耳朵聽。想到答案就按 🙋 搶答，大聲說出來！Can't see the word — just listen. Got it? Hit 🙋 Buzz in and shout your answer!
