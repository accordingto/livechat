THE GAME

What's the game?這是什麼遊戲？

One player gets a secret word card and describes it in English so others can guess — but every forbidden word on the card is off-limits! 一個人拿到一張祕密單字卡，用英文讓大家猜出這個字——但卡片上的禁字一個都不能說！

No camera, no app download — just open your own private link on your phone. Simple English is all you need, and the struggle is half the fun. 😂 不用開鏡頭、不用下載 App，用自己的手機打開專屬連結就能玩。英文不用很好，越卡越好笑！

"It's a fruit… hard on the outside… it wears a crown— oops, can't say crown!" 「它是一種水果……外面硬硬的……頭上戴著一頂皇冠——啊！不能說皇冠！」

ROLES

Three roles every round每一輪三種角色

Clue giver描述者

Sees the secret word and describes it in English — avoiding every forbidden word.看著祕密單字，用英文描述它，避開所有禁字。

Referee裁判

Also sees the forbidden words. The moment the clue giver says one, hit 🚨 Taboo!也看得到禁字。描述者一說出禁字，馬上按 Taboo! 抓包！

Guessers猜題者

Can't see the word — just listen. Got it? Hit 🙋 Buzz in and shout your answer!看不到題目，只靠耳朵聽。想到答案就按搶答，大聲說出來！
