How to play
  1. Each card is a scene. The bomb holder lights the fuse on their own card (the host can do it too).
  2. Holding the bomb? Say one thing that belongs in the scene, then tap PASS. No repeats!
  3. If an answer repeats or doesn’t fit, anyone else can tap “Didn’t count” — the bomb goes back and that player must say another.
  4. The fuse is secret and random. Whoever holds the bomb when it explodes takes the card and starts the next one.
  5. After the last card, the fewest bomb cards wins.
