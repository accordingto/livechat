🏰 Trivia Conquest

Roll the dice, move around the board, and answer an English trivia question to claim the land — landing on someone else's territory? Answer right and steal it!

Players
Each player takes turns rolling the dice and moving their token around the board. Land on a plain tile → answer a trivia question. Answer correctly and it's yours — even if a rival already owns it. Corner tiles are safe zones with no question. Most territory when the board fills up wins!
