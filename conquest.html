🏰 Dare Conquest

Roll the dice, move around the board, and pull off a silly dare to claim the land — or land on a BONUS, LUCKY, or GIFT tile for a special effect!

Players
Each player takes turns rolling the dice and moving their token around the board. Land on a plain tile → do the dare shown on screen. Pull it off and the land is yours — even if a rival already owns it, so the board keeps changing hands even after every tile is claimed. A few tiles are special and can't be claimed: LUCKY swaps all your territory with another player's, BONUS hands you 3 random territories, and GIFT gives you every unclaimed territory. Whenever you're ready, hit End Game to see who's on top!
