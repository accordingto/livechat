🏰 Dare Conquest

Roll the dice, move around the board, and pull off a silly dare to claim the land — or land on a BONUS, LUCKY, or GIFT tile for a special effect!

Players
🗳️ Player Voting optional

Players open their own card link and vote ✅ / ❌ on every challenge — you still make the final call. Same room code and links as Secret Rule, Emotion Cards, Word Wolf and Forbidden Words.

Each player takes turns rolling the dice and moving their token around the board. Land on a plain tile → do the dare shown on screen. Pull it off and the land is yours — even if a rival already owns it, so the board keeps changing hands even after every tile is claimed. A few tiles are special and can't be claimed: START and LUCKY both swap all your territory with another player's, BONUS hands you 2 random territories, and GIFT gives you every unclaimed territory (or a swap too, once nothing's left unclaimed).
