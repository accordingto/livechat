🏰 Dare Conquest

Roll the dice, move around the board, and pull off a silly dare to claim the land — or land on a corner for a special effect!

Players
Each player takes turns rolling the dice and moving their token around the board. Land on a plain tile → do the dare shown on screen. Pull it off and the land is yours — even if a rival already owns it. Landing on a corner triggers a special effect instead: LUCKY swaps all your territory with another player's, BONUS hands you 3 random territories, and GIFT gives you every unclaimed territory. Most territory when the board fills up wins!
